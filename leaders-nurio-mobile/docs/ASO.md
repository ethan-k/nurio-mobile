# Nurio Study Leader ASO and Store Asset Plan

Nurio Study Leader is a challenger app with a narrow professional audience. Metadata should favor clarity and qualified discovery over broad download volume.

## Positioning

- Audience: approved and candidate Nurio Study leaders in Korea.
- Core promise: run today’s sessions, schedules, attendance, notifications, and earnings from one focused workspace.
- Primary category: Education.
- Apple secondary category: Productivity.
- Localizations at launch: Korean and English.

## Asset Requirements

| Asset | Apple | Google Play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App icon | 1024×1024, opaque | 512×512, max 1 MB |
| Phone screenshots | 1–10; ship 6 authentic 6.9-inch portraits | 2–8; ship 6 authentic 1080×1920 portraits |

5. Earnings — “Track earnings clearly” / “정산 내역을 투명하게”
6. Settings — “Your workspace, your preferences” / “프로필과 언어 설정까지”

## Screenshot Production Rules

- Capture the shipped app on simulator/emulator using the review account.
- Use populated but fictional data and redact personal email, phone, bank, payment-reference, and learner identity details.
- Keep the native status bar and real in-app UI. Do not generate or reconstruct UI.
- Add captions only in post-production outside the app screenshot; never cover controls or imply unavailable features.
- Export separate EN and KO sets. Do not place both languages on one image.
- Apple exports must have no alpha channel. Google phone screenshots should be 9:16 and no larger than 8 MB.
- Store outputs belong in `/Users/ws/es/business/nurioworkspace/nurio_appstore/nurio-study-leader/`, not this repository.

## Optimization After Launch

- Track product-page conversion separately for KO and EN.
- Ask for ratings only after a successfully completed leader session; never interrupt schedule editing or incident handling.
- Respond to actionable 1–3 star reviews within two business days.
- After sufficient traffic, test the first three screenshot orders. Run one Google experiment for at least seven days; use Apple Product Page Optimization only when sample size supports it.
- Do not keyword-stuff. Google indexes the full description; Apple indexes title, subtitle, and the hidden keyword field.
