## Google Play

- Short description: `Manage sessions, schedules, attendance, notifications, and earnings in one app.`

### Full description

Nurio Study Leader gives approved Nurio Study leaders one focused mobile workspace for the work that matters before, during, and after each session.

See today’s agenda and upcoming sessions, manage recurring weekly availability and date-specific schedule exceptions, review confirmed participants, record attendance, complete session operations, and follow leader-only actions and updates.

The Earnings workspace shows gross, withholding, and net totals with a clear ledger history. Settings includes your public leader profile, notification preferences, language selection, privacy information, support, and account controls.

Candidate leaders can use the same app to continue their application, invitation, onboarding, and readiness steps.

Key features:

- Today and upcoming session overview
- Weekly schedule and exact-date exceptions
- Regular and Pair Practice session operations
- Attendance and session completion
- Leader-only notifications and action reminders
- Earnings totals and history
- Public leader profile management
- Korean and English workspace

An approved Nurio Study leader account is required for operational features.
